# Eleftherios Zacharioudakis, PhD

**Cybersecurity · Cryptography · Dependable computing**

I study secure authentication and the reliability of computer systems. I am a Visiting Lecturer in Computer Science at the [American University of Beirut – Mediterraneo](https://aubmed.ac.cy/), based in Pafos, Cyprus. Previously, I was Assistant Professor of Cybersecurity at Neapolis University Pafos.

My PhD in Computer Engineering is from the National Technical University of Ukraine “Igor Sikorsky Kyiv Polytechnic Institute”.

## Research software

These projects connect research themes with reproducible implementations and explicit evaluation evidence.

| Project | Focus | What to explore |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **FaultGraph Lab** | Graph-logical fault tolerance and reliability | Published-model reproduction, independent truth oracles and exact finite checks for optimized representations. |
| **VeriSOC Research** | Evidence verification for security triage | Traceable observations, bounded investigation, claim checking and PostgreSQL tenant isolation. |
| **ProofMesh Research** | Workload identity and federated trust | Deterministic authorization policies, trust-bundle rotation, SPIFFE/SPIRE integration and explicit uncertainty. |

Each repository documents its assumptions, implementation status and limitations. Research prototypes and controlled experiments are distinguished from production deployment, independent scientific validation and claims of novelty. Original coauthorship and AI-assisted implementation are disclosed in the project documentation.

## Research interests

- **Authentication and cryptography:** secure user identification and zero-knowledge approaches.
- **Dependable systems:** graph-logical models, reliability and fault-tolerant computing.
- **Cyber defense:** evidence-based analysis, AI-assisted investigation and shared security operations.
- **Distributed computing:** secure IoT infrastructure and edge–cloud systems.
